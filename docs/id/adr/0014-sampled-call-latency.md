# ADR 0014: Sampled call latency with exact outcomes

Status: ACCEPTED / IMPLEMENTED_UNRELEASED, 2026-10-28. The user approved sampled latency in favor of throughput.

Supersede the exact per-call latency requirement in ADR-0011/0012. Preserve exact successful-ID and failure-reason counters, the original call-entry wait deadline, encoding, clock/lease checks, capacity and logical-time observation. Immutable `latency-sample-interval` defaults to 1024 and accepts powers of two from 1 through 1048576; 1 restores full latency collection. Validate it before worker allocation. This is an observation option, not an identity-format field or a live-reload policy.

`GeneratorMetrics` owns the immutable sampling mask and bounded counters/histograms. It chooses a Bernoulli sample with probability 1/interval at call entry, before the outcome is known, using JDK thread-local randomness. No sampler thread, per-generator thread registry, queue or timestamp cache is introduced. The entry timestamp is still taken for every call to preserve its total wait budget. Only selected completions read the end timestamp and record duration; unselected successes increment a separate exact counter. Selected successful histogram counts plus unselected successes supply the exact generated count. Failures remain counted by reason regardless of selection. Do not unconditionally add failed durations to a sampled combined histogram, which would bias its population.

`IdSnapshot.getLatencySamples()` counts actual observed completions (successful and failed), and `getLatencySampleInterval()` describes the configured inverse probability, not a guarantee that exactly every Nth call is sampled. `getLatencyNanos()` is the sum of those observed durations only; do not scale it or divide it by all generated IDs. Percentiles are power-of-two bucket upper bounds over the sample. With zero samples, snapshot percentiles retain the legacy zero sentinel and consumers must check sample count; Micrometer exposes NaN, not a measured zero. Lifetime samples are not a sliding window or a statistically certified tail/SLO estimate. Concurrent snapshots remain approximate; counters are exact after writers complete.

Keep the existing factory/builder and metrics adapter. No strategy hierarchy is needed for one immutable sampling rule; the existing metrics owner keeps selection and aggregation together. Add sample count/interval meters, configuration metadata, binding and migration documentation. Full collection remains available for callers depending on the old cumulative-duration meaning. Random selection never participates in ID allocation.

Verification covers sampled/unselected mixed outcomes and duration totals under concurrency, exact full mode, zero-sample export, immutable/default/invalid configuration, both starter bindings, unchanged waiting/clock/ownership regressions, and frozen before/after plus Baidu throughput evidence with host-condition observations.
